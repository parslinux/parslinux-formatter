## 1. Nature of the Software and Destructive Formatting Warning

ParsLinux Formatter is a disk partitioning and formatting utility designed to repartition and format removable USB storage devices.

**CRITICAL WARNING:** Disk formatting operations are inherently destructive to data residing on the target block device. All partition tables, filesystems, and data previously stored on the selected target disk will be irreversibly erased upon confirmation.
